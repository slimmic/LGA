# Content verification checklist

- [ ] Permission to publish and use council branding
- [ ] Official council name and approved logo
- [ ] Current leadership names, titles, portraits and biographies
- [ ] Official office address, telephone and monitored email
- [ ] Verified LGA history, boundaries, wards and communities
- [ ] Approved department names, responsibilities and contacts
- [ ] Current service requirements, fees, timelines and payment channels
- [ ] Approved news and public notices with dates and sources
- [ ] Real photographs with permission, captions and alt text
- [ ] Secure enquiry form, privacy notice, spam protection and message handling
- [ ] Domain ownership, HTTPS, backups and named site administrator
- [ ] Mobile, desktop, keyboard, screen-reader and link testing
