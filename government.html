Reported by state ministry · 14 November 2025

Barr. Vincent Nemieboka

Chairman, Ogu/Bolo Local Government Area

The Ministry reported that Barr. Nemieboka swore in six Supervisory Councillors following screening and confirmation by the 8th Legislative Assembly of the Local Government. In remarks at the ceremony, he urged the councillors to carry out their duties diligently, work as a team and contribute to the development of Ogu/Bolo.

The same report said he announced plans for an empowerment programme intended for women, youths, first-class graduates, and students studying law, medicine and other professional disciplines, with the stated aim of building human capacity and self-reliance.

Source: Rivers State Ministry of Information & Communications — “Nimieboka Swears-in 6 Supervisory Councillors” (14 November 2025).

An official portrait, education and career history, dates in office, approved biography and current priorities have not been confirmed from this source. These have not been invented.
