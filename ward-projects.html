Looking ahead

Upcoming projects

Only projects with an official announcement or approval should be listed here. The cards below are tracking categories, not claims that a specific project has been approved.

Approved and scheduled

Projects with documented approval, a responsible office and expected timeline.

Announced / awaiting details

Publicly announced projects whose schedule, funding or ward coverage still needs confirmation.

Proposed community needs

Suggestions submitted by residents, clearly labelled as proposals and not approved commitments.

How to keep this page trustworthy: For every project, publish the ward/community, project description, status, date, responsible department, funding/contract details where public, expected completion date, progress photographs, and a source or official update.
